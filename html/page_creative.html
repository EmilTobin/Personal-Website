Creative Programming

Explore my creative coding projects made with OpenCV in C++ alongside Adobe tools. Most pictures and animations are created by comparing video frames and applying pixel-level effects based on differences in certain values. Adobe Premiere Pro is then used for final touches. I'm currently working on a project that brings these effects into a user-friendly program interface. You can follow its development through the link in the "Projects" section. No AI was used in the making of these projects. All base video footage is free to use and open-license materials.
